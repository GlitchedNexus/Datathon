# Datathon Slide Outline

1. Title & Team
   - Figure: optional hero image or logo
   - Takeaway: who you are and challenge tackled
2. Problem & Data Overview
   - Figure: data schematic / table preview
   - Takeaway: what data you received and key fields
3. Key Questions / Hypotheses
   - Figure: bullets
   - Takeaway: success criteria + target metric
4. EDA Highlights (1)
   - Figure: class/target distribution; `final_class_balance.png`
   - Takeaway: balance issues or skew
5. EDA Highlights (2)
   - Figure: correlation heatmap; `final_corr_heatmap.png`
   - Takeaway: strong signals/collinearity notes
6. Feature vs Target
   - Figure: top feature relationship; `final_feature_vs_target.png`
   - Takeaway: strongest driver(s)
7. Modeling Approach & Baselines
   - Figure: simple model diagram/table
   - Takeaway: baseline choices and rationale
8. Best Model(s) & Metrics
   - Figure: ROC/PR & confusion or error plot; `final_best_model_roc.png`
   - Takeaway: headline metric(s) + calibration note
9. Insights / Business Impact
   - Figure: small table/chart
   - Takeaway: actionable guidance
10. Limitations & Next Steps
    - Figure: bullets
    - Takeaway: risks and follow-up
11. Appendix
    - Figure: backups (feature importance, ablations)

Map each figure name to `figs/final/` and keep filenames descriptive and lowercase.
